    The part most tools skip

    It'll tell you when not to discount.

    The first instinct with a slow seller is to knock the price down. But a lot of products barely sell any faster when they're cheaper — and on a thin margin, a discount just hands away money. LihukAI checks that against your own price and cost before it suggests anything.

    Example · bacon
    Bundle it — don't discount
    Don't discount this one. Bacon does move faster when it's cheaper, but at your ₱3 margin a 15% cut would put you below cost. Pair it with something instead.

    A few things to know

    • It's a guide, not a guarantee. Every reading is a probability based on patterns in past sales, not a prediction about your specific shop. A product marked STALLED can still sell. One marked MOVING can still stall.
    • The training data isn't Philippine. The model learned from two years of a large foreign grocery chain's sales. The approach — how recently something sold, how steadily, how many people bought it — carries over fine. The specific category names, product pairings and discount responses may not.
    • The peso amounts are yours. Margin, cash tied up, and the discount check all come straight from the price and cost you type in. Any ₱ figure in the examples is made up.
    • This isn't financial advice. What you stock, discount or drop is your call. Factor in expiry dates, supplier terms and your own regulars — things this tool can't see.
    • Nothing you type gets sent anywhere. The checker runs entirely in your browser. No account, no tracking of your inputs, no server on the other end.
